Privacy Policy - Kampala Bee Run

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

Kampala Bee Run ("us", "we", or "our") operates the Kampala Bee Run mobile application and website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

1. Data We Collect

We collect: Name, Email, Phone Number, Race Category, and emergency contact info for race registration and communication.

2. How We Use Data

To manage race registration, send race updates via SMS/WhatsApp, generate bib numbers, and share results.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data may be shared with timing partners and medical teams only for race safety.

4. Data Security

We use encryption to protect participant data.
